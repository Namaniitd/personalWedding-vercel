# Hindu wedding photography attribution

These local 1920px renditions were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on
7 September 2026. They are resized copies of the listed source works, with no
other edits, and are kept locally so the invitation has no runtime dependency
on a third-party image host.

The `wedding-hands-960.webp`, `wedding-hands-1920.webp`,
`wedding-mehendi-960.webp`, and `wedding-mehendi-1440.webp` files are
resized WebP copies of their corresponding JPEGs for responsive delivery.

`../invitation-share.png` uses a tinted crop of `wedding-hands.jpg` as a generic
preview for private links. It contains no guest or wedding details.
