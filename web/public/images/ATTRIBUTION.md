# Artwork attribution

The invitation uses the following public-domain works, downloaded from
Wikimedia Commons on 3 September 2026 and kept locally so guests do not load
artwork from a third party while opening a private invitation.

- `ganesha-basohli.jpg` — *Ganesha, Basohli miniature* (c. 1730), anonymous
  artist. Public domain.
  Source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ganesha_Basohli_miniature_circa_1730_Dubost_p73.jpg>
- `gaja-lakshmi.jpg` — *Gaja Lakshmi* (c. 1780), unknown artist, British
  Museum collection. Public Domain Mark.
  Source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gaja_Lakshmi.jpg>

These images are decorative, respectfully labelled in the page markup, and are
not used to collect or transmit guest information.

The guest invitation and generic share preview also use Creative Commons
wedding photography. See [`wedding/ATTRIBUTION.md`](wedding/ATTRIBUTION.md)
for the photographers, licenses, source links, and share-preview crop.
